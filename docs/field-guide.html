Teaming Up! · field guide

There’s a committee in your head.

Emergenetics describes how you prefer to think (four colors) and how you behave (three spectrums). The patterns emerge from your genetic blueprint plus your life experience. They describe preferences, not talent: everyone can do everything, some things just cost more energy.

THINKING · 4 COLORS

Meet the committee members

Each color asks a different first question. Your profile shows how much energy you give each one.

COMIC · SAME NEWS, FOUR BRAINS

“Team, we’re changing the plan.”

Nobody is wrong. Each one is asking the question their brain asks first.

BEHAVIOR · 3 SPECTRUMS

How you show up

Behaviors are measured separately from thinking. Each is a 0–100 percentile split into thirds. The middle third is the “It Depends” crowd, who flex with the situation.

READING YOUR PROFILE

Two numbers, two different questions

≥23%

Percentage answers “how is my thinking energy split?” The four colors add up to 100%. Any color at 23% or more counts as a preference.

Percentile answers “how loud is it compared with everyone else?” It reflects the energy you spend on that kind of thinking or behaving.

32nd percentile: louder than about a third of people
95th percentile: louder than almost everyone

Two people can both be 30% Social. One may be at the 32nd percentile, the other at the 95th. Same mix, different volume.

CAN A PROFILE CHANGE?

Mostly stable, but yes

Profiles stay remarkably stable for years. Three things can shift them:

Conscious effortPracticing a non-preferred color on purpose, like a new degree, job or habit.
A significant life eventBecoming a parent, a loss, a move across the world.
Drugs or alcoholA temporary change. Please don’t take your profile after the office party.
